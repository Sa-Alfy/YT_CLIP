# The Clutch Cut — Weekly Top 10 (long-form countdown)

Locked 2026-11-24, from competitor research (ClipForge Sunday Top 10, Follow The Buck Top 20, Kyro multi-streamer compilations: 25–32x vs below-usual for single-creator).

## Why
- Shorts alone don't build YPP watch hours (Shorts Feed watch time doesn't count toward the 4,000 hours). A weekly narrated countdown funnels Shorts viewers into long-form.
- Multi-creator compilations massively outperform single-creator ones — and they give the channel a non-templated spine (YouTube's inauthentic-content risk is "clips with thin commentary or videos built from one template").
- Every Short already built becomes countdown material. No new VOD hunting for the first episodes.

## Format (locked)
- **Slot:** every Sunday 13:00 Dhaka (replaces that day's Daily Briefing slot). First episode: Sun 29 Nov 2026 13:00.
- **Length:** 8–12 minutes. 10 moments, countdown 10 → 1, best moment withheld to the end.
- **Structure per moment:** rank card (2s) → the Short's clip (25–35s, re-used from the already-built Short, not re-cut from the VOD) → narrator bridge (1 line: why it ranks here, what it beat) → next rank.
- **Narration:** same hybrid narrator voice as the Shorts. Original commentary throughout — never a raw clip compilation (YPP reused-content rule).
- **Credits:** every clip credited on screen (streamer name) + in the description with a Twitch link. Chapters with timestamps for all 10 ranks.
- **Title pattern:** "TOP 10 GTA 6 MOMENTS THIS WEEK — #1 WILL BREAK YOU" (vary the #1 tease per week; never promise what isn't in the video).
- **Description:** chapters, credits, subscribe line, #GTA6 #Gaming only.
- **Thumbnail:** ONE hero image (the #1 moment's payoff frame, face large), text 0–3 words, ONE accent colour, GTA VI logo small top-left. Same studied rules as Shorts thumbnails.
- **Pinned comment:** "Which moment should have been #1?" — reply to the first comments within 60 minutes.
