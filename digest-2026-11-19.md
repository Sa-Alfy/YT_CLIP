## Notes

- **Format: commentary/reaction clips (source audio only in v1; commentary mix is a defined later step — see README).** Straight re-uploads of creators' clips risk copyright strikes. Note: commentary/reaction is *more defensible*, not automatically safe — fair use, monetization/reused-content policy and Content ID are separate; there is no safe number of seconds, and attribution/short length are not protection. Written permission is the default for creators clipped regularly; keep a per-clip rights record.
- **v1 gap (from the 2026-11-19 GPT review): the commentary wiring in `clip.py` is not actually connected** — config `commentary.enabled`/`audio` is never read, so v1 clips are straight re-uploads in practice, the riskiest format. The commentary mix is a Phase 1 fix, awaiting Shariar's decision (approve v1.1 Phase 1 fixes or wait for v1 output first).
- Posting destination not chosen yet — clips are NOT posted anywhere.
- Composition: the gameplay panel is ~1/3 of the vertical frame height (horizontal 16:9 centred over a blurred fill). That is inherent to the horizontal→vertical conversion, not a resolution problem — the 720p cap stays.
